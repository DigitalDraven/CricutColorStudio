What to do next

  1. Open Cricut Design Space and start a new project. Choose Upload, then Upload Image, and pick the file you just downloaded.
  2. Put it on your canvas and check that the size is right. Each color is its own layer.
  3. Choose your material, press Make It, and follow the steps on the screen. Cut each color from its own sheet.
